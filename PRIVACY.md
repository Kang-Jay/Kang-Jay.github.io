# Visitor logging

This site records the visit time, requested page, a random browser identifier, IP address, and the country, region, and city inferred by Cloudflare from that IP address. The data is used only to understand portfolio traffic, is not displayed on the website, and is automatically deleted after 30 days. The random identifier remains in local storage until the visitor clears site data.

The site does not request GPS location or store query strings, form contents, cookies, or advertising identifiers in this log.
